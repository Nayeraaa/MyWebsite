Transformative Impact of Renewable Energy Access on Africa's Socio-Economic Development — Sudan Case Study

Supporting research on the socio-economic impacts of renewable energy access and adoption in Sudan, including employment, income generation, household welfare, entrepreneurship, and public service delivery. Examines the role of SMEs/MSMEs, financing mechanisms, and governance frameworks in an inclusive energy transition — with particular attention to youth, women, vulnerable groups, informal communities, and urbanization dynamics.
